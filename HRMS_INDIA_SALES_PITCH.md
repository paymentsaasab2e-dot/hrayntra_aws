# Framework HRMS — India Sales Pitch Guide

**Product:** Framework HRMS  
**Audience:** Founders, HR Heads, Ops Managers, CFOs of Indian SMEs / mid-market / multi-site companies  
**Use this for:** Discovery calls, demos, proposals, LinkedIn outreach, WhatsApp follow-ups

## How to use this document

Indian buyers rarely ask for “modules.” They ask about **pain**:

> “Staff bolta hai maine punch kar diya”  
> “Leave Excel pe chal raha hai”  
> “Laptop kiske paas hai pata nahi”  
> “New joiner ka CTC / PF structure delay ho jata hai”  
> “Field sales team kahan hai — no visibility”

**Pitch rule:** Lead with the problem → show the feature as the fix → close with the business outcome.

Talk features in the **order below**. That order matches what Indian buyers search for and what closes deals fastest.

## Feature → Problem → Solution (sales scripts)

### 1. Geofenced Attendance (Lead with this)

**What people search / say**
- Biometric alternative without hardware cost  
- “GPS attendance app for office”  
- “Staff remote se punch kar leta hai”

**Problem**
Manual registers, buddy punches, late marks fighting on WhatsApp, no proof for payroll disputes.

**Our solution**
- Check-in / check-out with GPS  
- Office geofence (lat/lng + radius per work location)  
- Remote punch only if permission is given  
- Late / half-day / present / absent statuses  
- HR can punch for exceptions; late marks go for approval  

**Demo line**
> “Agar employee office ke X meter ke bahar hai, punch reject — no debate.”

**Close**
Saves biometric machine cost + HR hours every month.

---

### 2. Leave Management (CL / SL / EL — India language)

**What people search / say**
- Casual leave, sick leave, earned leave software  
- Maternity / paternity leave tracking  
- Leave balance Excel replacement  

**Problem**
Balances wrong, approvals stuck with manager, LOP not recorded properly, festival rush chaos.

**Our solution**
- Apply leave self-service: Casual, Sick, Earned, Maternity, Paternity, LOP  
- Pending → Approved / Rejected with HR remark  
- Policy rules: annual quotas, carry-forward, advance notice, max consecutive days  
- Holiday calendar (public holidays)  

**Demo line**
> “Employee apply karta hai, manager / HR approve karta hai, balance auto update — Excel delete.”

---

### 3. Smart Onboarding (Hiring pain → 30-day retention)

**What people search / say**
- Employee onboarding checklist software India  
- Joiners laptop / email / policy delay  

**Problem**
Day-1 chaos: no CTC letter clarity, no laptop, no policy sign-off, manager not introduced → early exits.

**Our solution**
Hire pipeline with checklist %:
- Salary & CTC structure assigned  
- Hardware allocation  
- Email & workspace setup  
- Policy documentation signature  
- Induction + team introduction  
- Bulk import for campus / mass hiring  

**Demo line**
> “Har naya joiner ek tracked checklist pe — nothing falls through WhatsApp.”

---

### 4. India CTC & Compliance Structure (Trust builder)

**What people search / say**
- CTC calculator PF ESI HRA  
- Salary structure software for startups India  

**Problem**
Offer letters inconsistent; PF/ESI caps confusing; HR depends on CA Excel every time.

**Our solution**
- India Standard CTC profile (INR)  
- Earnings: Basic, HRA, Conveyance, Special Allowance  
- Deductions / contrib: Employee PF, Employer PF (with ₹1,800 style cap), ESI (employee / employer %), estimated TDS  
- Assign CTC at onboarding or employee profile  
- Live snapshot: Gross / Net / CTC  

**Honest pitch note**
> “Aaj CTC & structure ready hai. Full payslip run / bank transfer / Form 16 — next phase. Pehle structure sahi, phir payroll.”

**Why say it**
Indian buyers punish oversell. Honesty increases conversion.

---

### 5. Tasks & Projects (Manager’s daily workspace)

1. Kitne employees? Kitne offices / sites? Field team hai kya?

3. Leave kis type pe chalta hai — CL/SL/EL balance kaun manage karta hai?  
4. Laptop / phone allocation ka record kahan hai?  
5. Offer / CTC kaun banata hai — HR ya CA Excel?

7. Exit pe sabse badi headache kya hai?  
8. Multi-company / client companies manage karni hain?

Map answers → pitch order above. Don’t demo everything; demo their top 3 pains.

## Internal note for sales team

**Do pitch aggressively:** Attendance, Leave, Onboarding, Assets, Tasks, Field, Exit, Policy, Reports, India CTC structure.  

**Do not overclaim:** Full payroll processing, statutory filings (Form 16 / ECR), native mobile apps — say “roadmap” unless status changes.

**Conversion tip:** Always end the call with *one* measurable win for *their* company, e.g.  
> “Is mahine se leave Excel band, attendance disputes kam, exit pe asset checklist automatic.”
